# Data provenance and retrieval

The benchmark is derived from public collections including UCR time series, OpenML, TU graph datasets, TorchVision datasets, GLUE text data, and scikit-learn datasets. Raw third-party datasets are not redistributed because their original licences and terms remain authoritative.

The exact 60/183 partition is in `results/benchmark/eligible_partition_60dev_183test.csv`. The three additional neural-representation datasets and their metadata are in `results/neural/neural_dataset_metadata.csv`.

The derived result matrices included in this repository are sufficient to audit the reported rankings, tables, figures, ablations, and statistical comparisons. A separate archival URL for the complete large working outputs is pending.
